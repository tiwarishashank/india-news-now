This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Tuesday, November seventeenth.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has joined President Donald Trump and the prime ministers of Australia and Japan in Manila for the first in-person Quad summit since twenty twenty-four. Mr. Modi called the Quad a constructive and trusted partnership. The leaders announced a business leaders' forum, an artificial intelligence initiative, and new cooperation on maritime and cyber security. Earlier, at the ASEAN-India Summit, Mr. Modi proposed nine measures, including a long-term maritime cooperation plan and free e-visas for tourists from ASEAN countries.

In Uttarakhand, a passenger bus broke through a railing and plunged into a gorge about one hundred metres deep in Tehri Garhwal district early Tuesday. Officials told PTI at least eight people were killed and twenty-six injured.

Bilkis Bano has left Gujarat with her family, her uncle says, after the Supreme Court granted bail to two of the eleven men convicted in the two thousand two gang rape case, and suspended their life sentences. In a statement through her lawyer, Ms. Bano said she was neither informed nor heard before the order was passed, and she vowed to challenge it.

Delhi got some relief from smog. The Commission for Air Quality Management lifted Stage Three pollution curbs after the daily average Air Quality Index improved to two hundred and four, from two hundred and seventy-five on Monday. Stage Two rules stay in place.

On Dalal Street, stocks closed at their highest since late February, helped by record foreign inflows and lower oil prices. The Sensex rose four hundred and twenty-seven points to eighty-two thousand, five hundred and thirty-two. The Nifty gained ninety-six points to twenty-five thousand, three hundred and eighty-seven. The rupee jumped forty-six paise to ninety-three point four eight to the dollar, a seven-month high.

In entertainment, Ramayana Part One has crossed eight hundred crore rupees net in India. Meanwhile, the release of Sunny Deol's film Suryaa, set for November twentieth, has been postponed while the makers await certification, after medical associations objected to its portrayal of doctors.

And in cricket, Mahendra Singh Dhoni has retired from the Indian Premier League. Chennai Super Kings announced on retention deadline day that he will become the team's mentor. Dhoni led the franchise to five IPL titles and is its most-capped player, with two hundred and seventy-two appearances.
