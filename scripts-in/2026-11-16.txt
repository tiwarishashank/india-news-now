This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Monday, November sixteenth.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has arrived in Manila for the ASEAN-India Summit and the East Asia Summit, both on Tuesday. He later attended a gala dinner hosted by Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Junior. The White House says President Donald Trump will join a Quad leaders' meeting in Manila on Tuesday evening with Mr. Modi and the prime ministers of Australia and Japan. It would be the first Quad leaders' meeting in two years.

The number of Indian students in the United States has fallen for the first time in three years. The Open Doors report, released Monday, says the total dropped six and a half percent to about three hundred and thirty-nine thousand. Indian graduate students fell more than a third, while those on Optional Practical Training rose almost twenty-seven percent. India remains the largest source of international students in America.

Sonia Gandhi has formally launched her memoir, Belonging: A Journey of Love, in New Delhi, with Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi Vadra beside her. Opposition leaders, including Akhilesh Yadav, attended. Mrs. Gandhi said she wrote the book because so much said about her family is unreal. Publisher HarperCollins says two lakh copies have sold in under a week.

The storm in the Bay of Bengal is expected to strengthen into Cyclone Akvan. The India Meteorological Department says it could make landfall on the Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Tuni around noon on November twentieth, as a very severe cyclonic storm with winds of up to one hundred and thirty kilometres an hour, gusting to one hundred and forty-five.

On Dalal Street, foreign fund inflows and softer crude prices lifted stocks. The Sensex rose five hundred and twelve points to eighty-two thousand, one hundred and five, and the Nifty gained one hundred and forty-seven points to twenty-five thousand, two hundred and ninety-one. The rupee strengthened twenty-seven paise to ninety-three point nine two to the dollar.

In entertainment, Ramayana Part One keeps climbing. The Indian Express reports the film netted seventy-five crore rupees in India on its second Sunday, taking its domestic total to about seven hundred and sixty-one crore, and its worldwide gross to one thousand, three hundred and eighty-four crore in eleven days.

And in cricket, Jasprit Bumrah is back in India's Test squad for the two-match series in New Zealand, which starts Thursday at the Basin Reserve in Wellington. Rishabh Pant has been declared fit. The BCCI has released spinner Kuldeep Yadav to play domestic cricket, and called up pacer Gurnoor Brar for the first time after he led the wicket-taking chart in the one-day series.
