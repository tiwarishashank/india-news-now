This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Thursday, November twelfth.

Security forces in Jammu and Kashmir have recovered the bullet-riddled bodies of two Special Police Officers in Kishtwar district. Police named them as Rachpal Kumar and Bobby Chand. The two went missing on Wednesday from a border area, and their bodies were found in the Dacchan forest with their service weapons gone. It came hours after three CRPF personnel were wounded in a gun attack on a checkpoint in Kulgam.

In Uttar Pradesh, AIMIM chief Asaduddin Owaisi and Azad Samaj Party leader Chandra Shekhar Azad have announced a new alliance, the Samajik Parivartan Morcha, for next year's Assembly election. Owaisi named Azad, the Lok Sabha member from Nagina, as the alliance's chief ministerial candidate, and said the front aims to stop the BJP from winning a third straight term in the state.

In Hyderabad, Telangana Police say they rescued forty-three women from an alleged illegal egg extraction racket. The Indian Express reports the women were lured with promises of money while struggling with debt, illness and poverty. Investigators are probing links to twenty-three IVF centres, and say the operation may have run for two years.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will travel to Manila from November sixteenth to eighteenth for the ASEAN Summit. On Friday, he flags off the new Bengaluru to Mangaluru to Madgaon Vande Bharat Express.

Indian stocks edged higher for a third straight session on foreign buying and softer crude. The Sensex rose thirty-two points to eighty-one thousand, nine hundred and eleven, and the Nifty gained forty-four points to twenty-five thousand, two hundred and ninety-nine. The rupee weakened to ninety-four point zero four to the dollar, from ninety-three point six nine on Wednesday.

In entertainment, Ramayana Part One has crossed five hundred crore rupees in India in its first week. Hindustan Times reports the film netted about seventy-one crore on Wednesday, its first day without a festival holiday, taking its domestic total to five hundred and eleven crore. Its four dubbed versions have together crossed one hundred crore, a first for a Hindi film.

In cricket, India are into the final of the Under-Nineteen Asia Cup in Oman. They beat Afghanistan by eight wickets, chasing two hundred and twenty-eight with more than thirteen overs to spare. Captain Lakshya Raichandani made ninety-three, Aaryavir Sehwag added fifty-one, and V K Vineeth finished unbeaten on fifty-seven. India now face Pakistan in the final on Saturday, November fourteenth.
