India says it does not have structural excess capacity in any sector, pushing back on the United States after Washington proposed a tariff of up to twenty-five percent on Indian goods. The Commerce Ministry said it is examining the U.S. Trade Representative's notice, that India engaged constructively throughout the investigation, and that it remains committed to an early conclusion of the India-U.S. bilateral trade agreement, which would cut the proposed rate to seventeen percent.

A boardroom fight at India's biggest business group is headed to court. Tata Trusts have filed two suits in the Bombay High Court challenging the reappointment of N Chandrasekaran as executive chairman of Tata Sons for a third five-year term. The Trusts argue the decision broke the company's rules because the two directors they nominated disagreed, and they want to block a chairman's casting vote before a Tata Sons board meeting on November thirtieth. Justice Abhay Ahuja will hear the case on Friday.

In Uttar Pradesh, Akhilesh Yadav has released the Samajwadi Party's manifesto for the twenty twenty-seven Assembly election. It promises twenty-five lakh government jobs over five years, five thousand rupees a month for unemployed graduates, an immediate farm loan waiver, a legal guarantee of minimum support prices, a caste census, and the reopening of twenty-six thousand primary schools.

The Supreme Court has ruled that packaged food in India must carry separate front-of-pack warning labels for high fat, high sugar and high salt, rather than the single combined label the food regulator had proposed. Health activists called it a landmark ruling. It is a setback for food companies such as PepsiCo, Nestle and Coca-Cola, which had opposed the labels.

India made its pitch for the twenty thirty-six Olympics on Thursday. A delegation led by P T Usha, with P V Sindhu, I O C member Nita Ambani and Sports Minister Mansukh Mandaviya, gave a thirty-minute virtual presentation to the I O C's Future Host Commission, centred on Ahmedabad, which hosts the twenty thirty Commonwealth Games. India is one of seven countries in talks, and the process is expected to move to its next phase in March twenty twenty-seven.

On Dalal Street, stocks rose for a second day, led by technology shares. The Sensex gained three hundred and ninety points, or nearly half a percent, to eighty-four thousand, three hundred and twenty-nine. The Nifty added sixty-five points to twenty-five thousand, eight hundred and eighty-five. The Nifty I T index jumped more than three and a half percent. The rupee slipped back from an eight-month high, settling at ninety-two point four four to the dollar, as Brent crude climbed above ninety-six dollars a barrel.

In entertainment, Ramayana: Part One is slowing but still closing in on a milestone. Hindustan Times reports the Ranbir Kapoor and Yash film collected nine point six crore rupees on its third Wednesday, its weakest day so far, taking its India net total to about nine hundred and eighty-two crore rupees.

And in sport, India A posted three hundred and seventeen for eight against Sri Lanka A in the Asia Cup Rising Stars semifinal in Doha, with Abhishek Sharma making seventy-eight and Tilak Varma and Shahbaz Ahmed adding half-centuries. The senior team begins the second and final Test against New Zealand in Christchurch on Friday, local time, with captain Shubman Gill hinting at an unchanged side. And in Geneva, D Gukesh and Javokhir Sindarov drew game two of the World Chess Championship in twenty moves, leaving the match level at one-all.
