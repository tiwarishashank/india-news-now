Opposition protests over India's voter roll revision continued in Delhi on Wednesday. Student groups marched toward Jantar Mantar demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar, while the Cockroach Janta Party called for a Tiranga march at Connaught Place on October sixteenth. Delhi Police have registered FIRs over Monday's INDIA bloc protest march, citing lack of permission and alleged damage to barricades. Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta defended the Election Commission's Special Intensive Revision, saying such exercises have been conducted under governments of all parties. Petitions challenging the Commission, including one filed by Rahul Gandhi and other opposition leaders, may be heard by the Supreme Court later this month.

In a major business development, the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved the sale of a sixty point seven two percent stake in IDBI Bank to a Canadian subsidiary of Fairfax Financial Holdings for about fifty-two thousand, eight hundred and seventy-eight crore rupees. The deal covers thirty point four eight percent held by the government and thirty point two four percent held by Life Insurance Corporation of India, and would transfer management control to Fairfax subject to Reserve Bank of India approval. After the sale, the government and LIC would retain a combined thirty-four percent. Officials called it the largest foreign direct investment in India's financial sector so far.

Indian stocks slipped on Wednesday. The Sensex fell two hundred and fifty-two points, or about a third of a percent, to close at seventy-two thousand, seven hundred and eighty-two. The Nifty dropped ninety-nine points to end at twenty-two thousand, six hundred and twenty-three. Financial and pharma shares led the decline, while Nestle India and Infosys were among the gainers. Wholesale price inflation rose to eleven point three nine percent in September. The rupee gained nineteen paise to close at ninety-five point one seven against the U.S. dollar, its fourth straight session of gains, helped by Reserve Bank measures to curb speculative bets.

In entertainment, the ninth and final season of Koffee With Karan premieres Wednesday evening on JioHotstar, with Salman Khan as the first guest. Karisma Kapoor is also expected to appear. Separately, the Bombay High Court declined to grant an interim injunction against JioStar's upcoming Kangana Ranaut film Queen Forever, rejecting a plea by Phantom Studios, which alleges the project infringes its rights in the twenty fourteen hit Queen.

And in cricket, India lead West Indies two to one heading into the fourth T twenty in Hyderabad. Captain Shreyas Iyer won the toss and chose to bowl at the Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium. India are unchanged. The fifth and final match of the series follows later this week.
