This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Friday, November thirteenth.

The External Affairs Ministry says India has a national policy on Pakistan and expects everyone to follow it. Spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal was asked about an invitation to Punjab Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann to be chief guest at an international Punjabi conference in Lahore on November twenty-seventh. Jaiswal said political clearance for such trips is assessed in line with that policy, and that India's position on Pakistan after the Pahalgam terror attack should be amply clear.

Retail inflation has crossed the Reserve Bank's upper tolerance limit of six percent for the first time in two years. Government data show consumer prices rose six point zero nine percent in October, up from five point five four percent in September. Food inflation climbed to almost nine percent, with onion prices more than doubling from a year ago. Economists now expect the central bank to raise interest rates again at its meeting next month.

That inflation worry, along with high crude prices, ended a three-day rally on Dalal Street. The Sensex fell three hundred and nineteen points to eighty-one thousand, five hundred and ninety-two, and the Nifty lost one hundred and fifty-five points to twenty-five thousand, one hundred and forty-four. The rupee slipped twenty-one paise to ninety-four point two four to the dollar.

The Reserve Bank has given Paytm's payments arm in-principle approval to issue prepaid wallets, more than two years after curbs on Paytm Payments Bank shut its wallet business down. Paytm cannot relaunch yet. It must first pass a system and cybersecurity audit within six months and win final authorisation. Its shares briefly topped their twenty twenty-one listing price for the first time.

In entertainment, Ramayana Part One has crossed one thousand crore rupees worldwide. The Indian Express reports the film netted fifty-three and a half crore in India on its eighth day, taking its domestic total to about five hundred and sixty-five crore, and its global gross to one thousand and thirty-three crore. And Gujarat has issued an advisory to Diljit Dosanjh ahead of his November twenty-first concert in Ahmedabad: no songs about alcohol or drugs, and no calling children on stage.

In cricket, New Zealand crushed India by two hundred and forty-four runs in the fourth one-day international at Mount Maunganui. Will Young made a career-best one hundred and twenty-five as New Zealand posted three hundred and thirty-three. Then Matt Henry removed Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli and captain Shreyas Iyer, all for ducks, in the very first over. India were bowled out for eighty-nine, their lowest one-day total in fifteen years, and Henry finished with six for twenty-seven. India still lead the series two-one, with the final match on Sunday.
