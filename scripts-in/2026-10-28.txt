This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Wednesday, October twenty-eighth.

Cyclone Arnab has crossed the Andhra Pradesh coast and is now weakening. The India Meteorological Department says the very severe cyclonic storm made landfall near Narsapur in West Godavari district early Wednesday, with winds gusting to one hundred and fifty kilometers an hour, before weakening into a severe cyclonic storm over land. The Hindu reports five people have been killed in the state. Chief Minister Chandrababu Naidu has announced five lakh rupees for each victim's family, along with relief for damaged homes and farms. More than eighty-four thousand people had been moved to shelters.

The Congress drew a blank in elections to twenty-eight legislative council seats across Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar and Maharashtra. The BJP and the Janata Dal Secular swept all four seats in Karnataka, where the Congress governs, while in Uttar Pradesh the BJP won five seats and the Samajwadi Party three. Prime Minister Narendra Modi mocked the Congress over the result, noting that the polls used paper ballots.

The Union Cabinet has approved a three percent increase in dearness allowance and dearness relief, effective from July first, raising the rate from sixty to sixty-three percent. It will benefit more than one point one seven crore central government employees and pensioners.

Indian stocks fell, dragged down by Reliance Industries and technology shares, as crude oil climbed and investors waited on the U.S. Federal Reserve. The Sensex dropped three hundred and fifty-three points to seventy-three thousand, two hundred and forty-six, recovering most of a much steeper fall earlier in the day. The Nifty lost fifty-one points to twenty-two thousand, six hundred and ninety-nine. The rupee weakened twenty-one paise to ninety-five point two eight to the dollar. Jio Platforms made a strong market debut, ending nearly twenty-seven percent above its issue price and becoming India's second most valuable listed company after its parent, Reliance.

In entertainment, Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana, starring Ranbir Kapoor as Rama and Yash as Ravana, has sold two point six two lakh tickets in India ahead of its November fifth release, according to the Indian Express. Overseas advance sales have topped two million dollars, led by North America. Trade estimates suggest it could deliver the biggest opening of Ranbir Kapoor's career.

In cricket, India's women swept Zimbabwe three-nil in their one-day series, winning the third match by eight wickets. Vaishnavi Sharma took five wickets, and Pratika Rawal and Shafali Verma both made unbeaten fifties. And fast bowler Prasidh Krishna will miss the five-match one-day series against New Zealand starting November fourth with a hamstring injury. Jammu and Kashmir pacer Auqib Nabi is in contention to replace him.
