Police have arrested three men accused of firing at Bollywood actor Tiger Shroff's car in Mumbai. The shooting happened at about seven on Sunday evening, as Shroff was leaving a football club in Juhu. The actor escaped without gunshot wounds, but his driver was hit in the hand. Mumbai's Crime Branch tracked the suspects to a train headed for Jaipur, and Rajasthan police detained all three at Kota railway station on Monday. Kota police say the men were hired by an associate of the Lawrence Bishnoi gang and were planning to flee to Nepal.

U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio arrives in Mumbai on Tuesday for talks with External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar on trade, energy, defence, and technology. Speaking in Uzbekistan on Monday, Rubio said the two countries are very close to finalizing a trade deal, and that the negotiations were taken into account when Washington waived sanctions tied to Russian oil. He will also visit Reliance's refinery complex in Jamnagar on Wednesday.

India's foreign ministry has rejected references to Jammu and Kashmir made at a meeting of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation in Baghdad, calling them malicious, biased, and false, and accusing Pakistan of orchestrating them. The ministry said the grouping has no standing to comment on India's internal matters.

Markets fell sharply on Monday. The Sensex dropped eight hundred and seventy-six points, or about one point two percent, to close at seventy-three thousand, eight hundred and fifty-three. The Nifty lost two hundred and fifty-seven points to end at twenty-two thousand, eight hundred and eighty-nine. Private banks led the decline after quarterly results from HDFC Bank and Axis Bank disappointed investors. HDFC Bank's profit rose less than five percent, to about nineteen thousand five hundred crore rupees. State-run lenders bucked the trend, with Punjab National Bank and Union Bank of India both gaining. The rupee slipped three paise to about ninety-four point nine two to the dollar. Markets are closed on Tuesday for the Dussehra holiday.

In cricket, the BCCI has named former left-arm spinner Pragyan Ojha chairman of the men's selection committee, succeeding Ajit Agarkar, whose term ended on October fourth. Former off-spinner Ramesh Powar, who previously coached the India women's team, joins the five-member panel. Ojha had served as interim chief and led the panel that picked the squads for India's all-format tour of New Zealand, which begins on Thursday.

And at the Asian Para Games in Japan, India won eleven medals on the opening day, including four golds. Mona Agarwal won the women's ten-metre air rifle standing event with a Games record, with Avani Lekhara taking silver. Nishad Kumar won gold in the men's T forty-seven long jump with a leap of seven point one one metres.
