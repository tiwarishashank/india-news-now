The fight over India's voter rolls widened on Friday. Punjab's Assembly unanimously passed a resolution seeking an independent inquiry into alleged electoral irregularities and the functioning of the Election Commission under Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann blamed the B J P for what he called vote loot, and demanded that the special intensive revision of electoral rolls be scrapped. In Delhi, around five hundred students, farmers and activists marched through the city center, demanding that the revision be rolled back and that the Chief Election Commissioner resign.

Two of Haryana's most wanted gangsters have been detained in the United States. Himanshu Bhau and Neeraj Faridpuria were picked up in Las Vegas after Indian agencies shared their location with American authorities. Bhau faces more than thirty cases, at least sixteen of them for murder or attempted murder. Indian agencies are now pressing for their deportation.

Indian stocks ended a bruising week lower. On Friday the Sensex fell about one hundred and twenty-six points to seventy-three thousand, one hundred and twenty-four, and the Nifty lost nearly eighty-eight points to close at twenty-two thousand, six hundred. Over the week, the Nifty fell two point four percent and the Sensex two point two percent, as Brent crude climbed to about one hundred and fourteen dollars a barrel after Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia. Small and midsize stocks had their worst week in twenty months. The rupee closed at ninety-four point nine three to the dollar.

The record Jio Platforms share sale closed with bids for just over twenty-two times the shares on offer. Institutional investors bid for more than seventy times their portion. The offering drew more than thirteen million applications, making it the first share sale anywhere to pass ten million.

At the box office, Ayushmann Khurrana and Sara Ali Khan's spy comedy Udta Teer opened to a modest four point one crore rupees in India on Friday, amid mixed reviews. Rajinikanth's Jailer Two has now earned more than six hundred and twenty-eight crore rupees worldwide in nine days.

In cricket, India beat New Zealand by four wickets in Christchurch on Saturday to take a two-nil lead in the five-match T twenty series. Chasing one hundred and eighty-one, India slumped to seventy for five, but Tilak Varma's unbeaten fifty-one off twenty-six balls and Nitish Kumar Reddy's unbeaten twenty-six sealed the win with one ball to spare. Their unbroken stand of sixty-nine is India's highest for the seventh wicket in T twenty internationals. Mitchell Santner took four for twenty-one, and Glenn Phillips made seventy for New Zealand. The third match is in Wellington on October twenty-seventh.
