Opposition leaders have taken their fight over India's voter rolls to the Supreme Court. Lok Sabha Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi, Rajya Sabha Leader of Opposition Mallikarjun Kharge and other INDIA bloc leaders filed a joint petition challenging the Election Commission's Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls. They are asking the court to make the Commission publish, booth by booth, the names of voters deleted during the revision, along with the reasons, and to suspend the exercise while the case is heard. The petition was registered on Tuesday. The BJP has hit back, with Union minister Piyush Goyal accusing the Congress of double standards.

In Delhi, police have removed Jantar Mantar's status as a designated protest site, following days of opposition protests in the capital. Three new sites have been named instead: Ramlila Maidan, Burari ground and CBD ground.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi spoke by phone with Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney on Tuesday. Mr. Modi called it a warm and productive conversation. According to Canada's readout, the two leaders recommitted to concluding a trade agreement at the earliest opportunity, with the aim of doubling two-way trade by twenty thirty. They agreed to stay in close contact ahead of Mr. Modi's visit to Canada in December.

Indian stocks had their best day in months. The Sensex jumped one thousand, one hundred and nine points, or about one and a half percent, to close at seventy-three thousand and thirty-three. The Nifty rose three hundred and fifty-nine points to end at twenty-two thousand, seven hundred and twenty-two. Technology stocks led the rally, with HCL Technologies up more than eight percent after reporting higher profit. Gains extended in the afternoon after Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said the government was open to hearing views on removing the long-term capital gains tax.

In entertainment, the makers of Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana unveiled Anupam Kher as the voice of Jatayu. The film stars Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Rama, and its first part releases worldwide on November fifth.

And in cricket, India lead West Indies two to one in their five-match T20 series after a fourteen-run win in Indore. India posted two hundred and twenty-three for five, helped by a late cameo from fast bowler Prince Yadav, who later revealed he'd used Abhishek Sharma's bat. The fourth match is on Wednesday in Hyderabad.
