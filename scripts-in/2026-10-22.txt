This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Thursday, October twenty-second.

An Indian national was killed and two others were injured in an attack on Dammam airport in Saudi Arabia, officials said on Thursday. It came a day after another Indian was hurt in a drone attack on the kingdom's Shaybah oilfield, amid a wave of strikes by Yemen's Houthi rebels on Saudi infrastructure. The Indian Embassy has urged Indians in Saudi Arabia to stay alert, follow local instructions, and take shelter indoors during aerial attacks.

Those attacks weighed on Indian markets. Stocks fell for a third straight session as oil climbed to about one hundred and twelve dollars a barrel. The Sensex lost four hundred and sixty points to close at seventy-three thousand, two hundred and forty-nine, and the Nifty dropped about one hundred and twenty-four points to twenty-two thousand, six hundred and eighty-eight. The rupee snapped an eight-session winning streak, weakening to close at ninety-four point eight two to the dollar.

In politics, leaders of the opposition INDIA bloc were set to meet by video on Thursday evening to plan their next steps in the row over Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. The opposition accuses the Election Commission of deleting eligible voters during the special revision of electoral rolls. The Commission denies wrongdoing. Meanwhile, Cockroach Janta Party leaders Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka, released from Tihar Jail on Wednesday, were summoned by the Delhi Police Crime Branch for questioning. They were freed after agreeing not to call protests at Jantar Mantar until it is again designated a protest site.

Home Minister Amit Shah said protests would achieve nothing, and predicted the National Democratic Alliance would form the next government in Punjab. And Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla said he would speed up a decision on the Trinamool Congress's petitions to disqualify twenty of its rebel members of Parliament.

In entertainment, Delhi Police have denied permission for Diljit Dosanjh's October thirtieth concert at Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium, citing security concerns and the festive season. In Mumbai, police have now made ten arrests over the October eighteenth firing at actor Tiger Shroff's car.

In cricket, India pulled off their highest successful run chase in T twenty internationals, beating New Zealand by four wickets in Christchurch to take a one-nil lead in the five-match series. New Zealand made two hundred and twenty-five for four, led by Glenn Phillips' unbeaten eighty-two off forty balls. India replied with two hundred and thirty for six, with two balls to spare. Sanju Samson hit sixty-seven off thirty-three balls, sharing an opening stand of ninety-three with Abhishek Sharma, and Shreyas Iyer and Naman Dhir each added forty-two.
