India's east coast is bracing for a powerful storm. The India Meteorological Department says a depression over the Bay of Bengal is likely to intensify into a very severe cyclonic storm and cross the Andhra Pradesh coast between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam on October twenty-eighth. Winds could reach one hundred and twenty-five kilometers an hour, gusting to one hundred and forty. Extremely heavy rain is forecast for coastal Andhra Pradesh, Odisha and Telangana, and fishermen have been told to stay off the sea until October twenty-ninth.

The fight over the voter roll revision continues. The Election Commission has refused to disclose the name and version of the software it used to flag errors in voter rolls during the special intensive revision, saying the information is a trade secret exempt from the Right to Information Act. The Congress accused the commission of hiding what it called massive, centralised manipulation. Rahul Gandhi mockingly thanked the B J P for promoting the opposition INDIA bloc's rally against alleged vote theft, set for November first.

For Indian travelers in the Gulf, more flight disruptions. Air India and Air India Express have suspended flights to Riyadh and Dammam until November seventh, and to Jeddah until October thirty-first, amid attacks at Saudi airports. IndiGo has cancelled Jeddah, Dammam, Madinah and Riyadh services until October thirty-first, and Akasa Air has halted Riyadh flights until November thirtieth.

Indian markets are closed for the weekend after a bruising week. On Friday the Sensex closed at seventy-three thousand, one hundred and twenty-four, and the Nifty at twenty-two thousand, six hundred, with the Nifty down two point four percent for the week as crude oil prices surged. The rupee ended at ninety-four point nine three to the dollar.

In entertainment news, police have recovered the two pistols used in the October eighteenth shooting at actor Tiger Shroff. They were found in bushes near the railway station in Surat, where the shooters had tossed them while on the run. Police also arrested the man accused of supplying one of the guns, the eleventh arrest in the case.

In cricket, India's women piled up three hundred and twenty-eight for six against Zimbabwe in the second one-day international in Vadodara on Sunday. Former captain Harmanpreet Kaur made one hundred and twenty-six, her eighth one-day hundred, and Deepti Sharma hit an unbeaten one hundred and six. The pair added one hundred and fifty-three for the fifth wicket. India's men lead New Zealand two-nil in their T twenty series, and New Zealand will rest pacers Lockie Ferguson and Matt Henry for the third match in Wellington on Tuesday, October twenty-seventh.
