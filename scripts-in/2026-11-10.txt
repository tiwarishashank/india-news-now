This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Tuesday, November tenth.

Sonia Gandhi's memoir, Belonging: A Journey of Love, was released on Tuesday, and it has already stirred a row. In the book, she writes that in twenty ten, Pranab Mukherjee "shocked" her by suggesting it was time for "a change at the top," which she understood as a hint of his long-held desire to become prime minister. His daughter, Sharmistha Mukherjee, hit back on social media, asking, "Is it a crime to be ambitious?" She said her father had risen on merit, not dynastic entitlement.

The External Affairs Ministry says the US midterm elections, in which Democrats won both houses of Congress, are an internal American matter. Spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said India-US ties enjoy strong bipartisan support. The ministry also condemned Chinese vloggers who allegedly filmed slums in Dhaka and passed them off as New Delhi, and urged Bangladesh to act.

In Ladakh, polling for all eight Autonomous Hill Development Councils will be held on December fourth.

Indian markets were shut on Tuesday for Diwali Balipratipada and reopen on Wednesday. On Monday, the Sensex closed at eighty-one thousand, one hundred and seven, and the Nifty at twenty-five thousand and forty-five. The rupee snapped a six-session winning streak, ending at ninety-three point nine three to the dollar. In corporate news, Blackstone will invest five thousand crore rupees in IIFL Finance for a stake of about sixteen percent, part of a six thousand, four hundred crore capital raise that still needs shareholder and regulatory approval.

In entertainment, Ramayana Part One had a record Monday. Hindustan Times reports the film netted one hundred and seven and a half crore rupees in India on its fifth day, Bollywood's biggest Monday ever, taking its domestic net to three hundred and forty-six crore. Its worldwide gross now stands at six hundred and sixty-three crore.

In cricket, rain washed out the third one-day international between New Zealand and India in Hamilton without a ball bowled. India still lead the five-match series two-nil. Shubman Gill and K L Rahul now join the Test squad in Wellington, and Shreyas Iyer will captain India in the last two games at Mount Maunganui, on November thirteenth and fifteenth. And at the Under-Nineteen Asia Cup, Aaryavir Sehwag, son of Virender Sehwag, hit one hundred and sixty off one hundred and sixty-eight balls as India beat the UAE by seventy runs to reach the semifinals. They face Afghanistan on November twelfth.
