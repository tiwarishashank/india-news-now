Former Deputy Prime Minister L.K. Advani is in intensive care at Apollo Hospital in Delhi with a chest infection. The ninety-eight-year-old B.J.P. veteran was admitted on Friday evening. In a statement on Saturday, the hospital said his condition is stable and that he remains under close medical observation. Union Health Minister J.P. Nadda visited him on Saturday.

The Supreme Court has held that access to and use of the internet is a fundamental right of every person, including children, irrespective of age. A bench of Justices J.B. Pardiwala and K. Vinod Chandran rejected blanket age-based bans, warning that they could undermine children's freedom and privacy, and their ability to seek help when they face abuse online. The court said proportionate safeguards, such as parental oversight and child modes, remain open.

In Delhi, Cockroach Janta Party founder Abhijeet Dipke was granted conditional bail on Saturday, a day after police detained him on his way to a protest at Connaught Place and then arrested him. A special executive magistrate released him on a bond, with a key condition that he cause no breach of peace for one year. Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis, meanwhile, accused the Opposition of seeking regime change through its protests against the revision of electoral rolls, a charge the Opposition rejects.

In business, the country's two largest private lenders reported quarterly results on Saturday. HDFC Bank's standalone net profit rose about four point seven percent to nineteen thousand, five hundred and ten crore rupees, narrowly missing analyst estimates, as net interest income grew ten percent. ICICI Bank's consolidated profit jumped about twenty-two percent to sixteen thousand, three hundred and thirty-three crore rupees. Markets are shut for the weekend. On Friday, the Sensex closed at seventy-four thousand, seven hundred and twenty-nine, and the Nifty at twenty-three thousand, one hundred and forty-six, while the rupee settled near ninety-four point nine six to the dollar.

Tamil cinema is mourning composer Deva, who died in Chennai on Saturday at seventy-five after a cardiac arrest. Deva composed the intro music that announces Rajinikanth in his films, and Rajinikanth arrived in tears to pay his respects, while Kamal Haasan remembered him as a dear brother. Rajinikanth's new release, Jailer Two, has crossed two hundred and thirteen crore rupees worldwide in two days, with forty crore from overseas markets on day two alone.

In cricket, rain delayed the toss in Bengaluru for the fifth and deciding T twenty between India and West Indies, with the series level at two-all. Separately, the B.C.C.I. named its squads for the coming New Zealand series and left Yashasvi Jaiswal out of the five-match one-day series starting November fourth, a call R. Ashwin described as unfair.
