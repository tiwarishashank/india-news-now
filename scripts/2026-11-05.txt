Union Home Minister Amit Shah on Thursday challenged Congress leader Rahul Gandhi to seek a special session of Parliament on the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls. Speaking at a BJP Loktantra Bachao rally in Karnal, Haryana, Shah said the government was ready for debate if Gandhi wrote to Prime Minister Narendra Modi. He also asked Gandhi to account for his recent Vietnam visit, including whom he met and why he went, and accused the Congress of disrupting Parliament and protecting infiltrators on voter lists. Separately, the Election Commission ordered a Special Summary Revision of electoral rolls in Arunachal Pradesh, Mizoram, Odisha, Sikkim, Chandigarh, and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, where an intensive revision has already been completed. Draft rolls are due November thirtieth, with final rolls on January twenty-first, twenty twenty-seven.

Indian stocks surged for a sixth straight session as crude oil prices fell on hopes of eased West Asia tensions after Democrats won control of the U.S. Congress. The Sensex jumped two thousand and nine points, or two point six percent, to close at seventy-nine thousand, three hundred and ten. The Nifty rose five hundred and seventy-six points, or two point four percent, to twenty-four thousand, five hundred and thirty-seven. Autos, realty, and financials led the rally. The rupee gained for a fifth session, settling at ninety-four point four seven to the dollar, its strongest level since October twenty-first.

In entertainment, Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana Part One opened worldwide on Thursday, with Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Rama, Sai Pallavi as Sita, and Yash as Ravana. Made on a reported budget of about sixteen hundred crore rupees, it is billed as the most expensive Indian film ever, with music by Hans Zimmer and A R Rahman. Advance bookings crossed one hundred and fifty crore rupees before release, and early reviews praised the scale and visual effects. By early evening, trade trackers had the India net near forty-six crore rupees.

In cricket, the BCCI shifted the first ODI between India and Sri Lanka from Delhi to Visakhapatnam. The December thirteenth match will now be played at the ACA-VDCA Cricket Stadium after the Delhi and District Cricket Association cited potential weather disruption and proposed renovation work at Arun Jaitley Stadium. Bengaluru and Ahmedabad still host the second and third ODIs on December sixteenth and nineteenth.
