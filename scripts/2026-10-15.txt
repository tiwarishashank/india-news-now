The Supreme Court on Thursday issued notices on a series of petitions challenging the Election Commission's Special Intensive Revision of voter rolls, and on the mass detentions of protesters and journalists in Delhi last week. Delhi Police told the court the detentions were preventive and lasted two to six hours. Separately, the Delhi High Court refused to stop the Cockroach Janta Party from holding its Tiranga protest at Connaught Place on Friday, while recording a police assurance that the event would be regulated. The party's founder, Abhijeet Dipke, alleged that more than thirteen crore voters had been struck off the rolls.

In another ruling, the Supreme Court held that a Hindu from a Backward Class cannot claim the reservation benefits available to Backward Class Muslims merely by converting to Islam. The court dismissed an appeal by the Tamil Nadu government defending its policy. Also Thursday, Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated five development projects in Ladakh worth more than three thousand, three hundred crore rupees.

On trade, Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal said the first tranche of the India-U.S. trade deal has been finalised, with only the final contours and legal vetting remaining. He said it would be implemented once there is clarity on India's competitive advantage over its peers. U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer has said he does not see a deal as imminent.

Indian stocks had their best day in four months. The Sensex jumped one thousand, five hundred and thirty-four points, or two point one percent, to close at seventy-four thousand, three hundred and sixteen. The Nifty rose three hundred and seventy-five points to end at twenty-two thousand, nine hundred and ninety-seven. Tech Mahindra, ICICI Bank and Reliance Industries each gained more than four percent, as Brent crude fell about two percent. Smaller stocks lagged, with the Nifty Smallcap one hundred index down one point three percent. The rupee rose twelve paise to close at ninety-five point zero five against the U.S. dollar, its fifth straight session of gains.

In entertainment, Rajinikanth's Jailer Two opened worldwide on Thursday in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada and Hindi, with the Hindi version on more than fifteen hundred screens in North India, though not in the national multiplex chains. And Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana, starring Ranbir Kapoor, Sai Pallavi and Yash, kicked off its international campaign Thursday with a second trailer, three weeks ahead of its global release.

In cricket, West Indies beat India by twenty-four runs in the fourth T twenty in Hyderabad to level the series two all. Chasing two hundred and forty-eight, India made two hundred and twenty-three in nineteen point three overs. Sanju Samson top scored with fifty-six, and Keemo Paul took five wickets. The decider is on Saturday at the Chinnaswamy Stadium in Bengaluru.
