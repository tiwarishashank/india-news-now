This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Sunday, November first.

India's opposition took its fight with the Election Commission to the streets of Delhi on Sunday. The INDIA bloc held a "Chalo Dilli" rally at Ramlila Maidan, a day after its symbolic public impeachment of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Rahul Gandhi, Sonia Gandhi, Mallikarjun Kharge, Akhilesh Yadav and Mamata Banerjee were among the leaders who addressed the crowd over alleged irregularities in the special revision of voter rolls. Rahul Gandhi called the alleged vote theft treason and said Kumar would not survive in office. Kharge said removing Prime Minister Narendra Modi was essential to save democracy. Organisers had projected up to ten lakh people, while Delhi Police expected about fifty thousand. The BJP's Sambit Patra said around forty percent of the ground was empty, and the party held a counter rally in Rohtak, Haryana.

On the economy, festive shopping lifted tax collections. Gross GST revenue rose nearly twenty-three percent from a year earlier to about two point three one lakh crore rupees in October, the second-highest monthly haul on record and the second straight month above two lakh crore. Revenue from imports jumped forty-two percent. Net collections after refunds rose twenty-six percent, to more than two lakh crore rupees.

Fuel costs moved up again on Sunday. Oil companies raised the price of a nineteen-kilo commercial LPG cylinder by ten rupees, to two thousand eight hundred and twenty rupees in Delhi. Jet fuel rose by one rupee thirty-seven paise a litre, to one hundred and thirty-eight rupees fifty paise, its fourth straight monthly increase. Household cooking gas, petrol and diesel prices are unchanged.

Markets were closed for the weekend. On Friday the Sensex ended at seventy-four thousand, four hundred and fifty-nine, and the Nifty at twenty-three thousand and fifty-three, both up nearly two percent for the week. The rupee closed at ninety-four point nine three to the dollar.

In entertainment, Ranbir Kapoor and Yash's Ramayana Part One is four days from release. Tracker Sacnilk says advance bookings in India have crossed thirty-six crore rupees for the first two days, with more than eight lakh tickets sold. Overseas pre-sales are about three point eight million dollars, with North America accounting for nearly three-quarters. The film opens Thursday, November fifth.

In cricket, New Zealand beat India by seven wickets in the fifth and final Twenty20 International in Hamilton, but India take the series three to two. Ishan Kishan made sixty-one and captain Shreyas Iyer fifty as India posted one hundred and ninety-one for eight. Tim Seifert hit ninety-three and Glenn Phillips an unbeaten sixty-five as New Zealand chased it down with two overs to spare. Adam Milne took four wickets. The one-day series begins Wednesday in Auckland.
