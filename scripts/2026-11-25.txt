The United States has proposed a tariff of up to twenty-five percent on all Indian goods, after concluding two Section three-oh-one trade investigations, one into goods made with forced labour and one into what Washington calls excess industrial capacity. The U.S. Trade Representative said the rate would fall to seventeen percent if India and the United States sign a formal trade deal. That would leave India slightly better placed than Bangladesh, Indonesia, Malaysia and Thailand, which face eighteen percent, and Vietnam, at twenty percent. The proposal is open for public comment, and a hearing will follow. India and thirteen other economies face fresh duties as President Trump rebuilds his tariff wall.

At home, the Union Cabinet approved projects worth nearly twenty thousand crore rupees. They include an eleven thousand crore rupee scheme to boost domestic manufacturing of construction equipment such as tunnel boring machines and excavators, a second container terminal at Kamarajar Port in Tamil Nadu, and a new greenfield airport at Doloo in Assam.

In Uttar Pradesh, the seat-sharing row between the Samajwadi Party and the Congress has turned public. The Congress's state in-charge, Rajendra Pal Gautam, said there is no point in contesting fewer than one hundred of the state's four hundred and three seats, and called Akhilesh Yadav's talk of twenty-six seats irresponsible. Congress lawmaker Imran Masood said, "We are not beggars." The Samajwadi Party has already named five candidates.

India has declined to comment on Sheikh Hasina's plan to return to Bangladesh on December sixteenth. External Affairs Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal called it a matter between the former prime minister and the Bangladesh government, and said a citizen returning voluntarily on a travel document is separate from Dhaka's extradition request, which India is still examining.

India makes its pitch for the twenty thirty-six Olympics on Thursday. Sports Minister Mansukh Mandaviya and P T Usha will lead a virtual presentation to the International Olympic Committee, joined by badminton star P V Sindhu, with Ahmedabad as the proposed host city. India is one of seven countries in talks with the I O C. And New Delhi will host badminton's Thomas and Uber Cup finals in twenty twenty-eight.

On Dalal Street, markets roared back after the holiday. The Sensex jumped one thousand five hundred and two points, or one point eight two percent, to eighty-three thousand, nine hundred and thirty-eight. The Nifty rose four hundred and seventy points to twenty-five thousand, eight hundred and nineteen. Hindustan Unilever led the gains, up nearly six percent. Analysts credited continued foreign buying and Brent crude slipping below ninety dollars a barrel. The rupee climbed to an eight-month high, closing at ninety-two point two three to the dollar.

In entertainment, Ramayana: Part One is closing in on one thousand crore rupees at the Indian box office. Hindustan Times reports the Ranbir Kapoor, Yash and Sai Pallavi film has collected about nine hundred and seventy-three crore rupees net in India in twenty days, helped by cut ticket prices that lifted its third Tuesday above Monday.

And in sport, the second and final Test between India and New Zealand begins Friday at Hagley Oval in Christchurch, on a pitch expected to help bowlers more than Wellington did. New Zealand have called up Matt Fisher for the injured Blair Tickner. India A face Sri Lanka A in the Asia Cup Rising Stars semifinal in Doha on Thursday. In Geneva, D Gukesh drew the opening game of his World Chess Championship defence against Javokhir Sindarov, and game two is under way.
