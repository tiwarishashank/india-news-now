Delhi and the National Capital Region moved to Stage Three of the Graded Response Action Plan on Friday as air quality edged toward the severe category. The Commission for Air Quality Management imposed the tighter curbs, which limit construction dust, restrict older diesel vehicles, and step up other emergency measures ahead of Diwali. The Delhi government also appealed to residents to burst only certified green firecrackers, and only during the narrow windows allowed by the Supreme Court on November seventh and eighth.

Separately, the Election Commission said voters marked Absent, Shifted, Dead, or Duplicate in the seven states still undergoing Special Intensive Revision can now refill their Enumeration Forms to get back onto the final rolls, instead of filing Form Six as first-time voters. Those electors had been left off draft rolls after their forms were issued but not returned. In Jammu and Kashmir, National Conference president Farooq Abdullah met seventeen dissident legislators and ruled out any party split, saying their genuine concerns would be addressed step by step.

Indian stocks extended their winning streak to a seventh session, defying a global sell-off in technology shares. The Sensex jumped one thousand seven hundred and eighteen points, or two point two percent, to close at eighty-one thousand and twenty-eight. The Nifty rose four hundred and ninety-four points, or two percent, to twenty-five thousand and thirty, reclaiming the twenty-five thousand mark for the first time since February. Banks led the rally amid festive optimism. The rupee surged fifty-two paise to close at ninety-three point eight nine to the dollar, helped by a softer greenback and firm domestic markets.

In entertainment, Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana Part One collected about one hundred and fifty-five crore rupees worldwide on its opening day, which the makers called the highest pre-Diwali Day One for an Indian film. Ranbir Kapoor plays Lord Rama, with Sai Pallavi as Sita and Yash as Ravana. Separately, Salman Khan announced that his delayed war drama Maatrubhumi will finally release in theatres on December fourth.

In cricket, India takes a one-nil lead into Saturday's second ODI against New Zealand in Wellington after Virat Kohli's seventy-four steered a five-wicket win under the Duckworth-Lewis method in the rain-hit opener at Auckland. Meanwhile, Rishabh Pant has been ruled out of the third India A four-day game in New Zealand with a side strain, as he continues rehabilitation in Bengaluru ahead of the Test series.
