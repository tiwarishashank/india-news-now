A helicopter crashed in Uttarakhand on Friday afternoon, killing all six people on board, including the pilot. The Trans Bharat Aviation helicopter was flying from Badrinath to Dehradun when it went down near Joshimath in Chamoli district amid rain and heavy cloud. Officials say it lost contact shortly after takeoff.

In Delhi, rain has forced the opposition INDIA bloc to postpone its so-called public impeachment of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar to Saturday, October thirty-first. The protest targets the special intensive revision of electoral rolls, which the opposition says is disenfranchising voters. Rahul Gandhi says the bloc's rally at Ramlila Maidan on November first will go ahead. Prime Minister Narendra Modi, speaking in Gujarat, accused the opposition of spreading rumours about fertiliser and gas shortages during the Gulf crisis to create panic.

Rahul Gandhi also criticised the planned sale of IDBI Bank, saying public banks are tools to build the nation, not assets to be sold. The government has approved a unit of Canada's Fairfax buying about sixty-one percent of the bank, a deal still awaiting Reserve Bank of India clearance.

Indian stocks rose for a second day. The Sensex gained three hundred and eight points to seventy-four thousand, four hundred and fifty-nine, and the Nifty climbed one hundred and sixteen points to close above twenty-three thousand, at twenty-three thousand and fifty-three. Infosys and other IT shares led, while mid and small caps fell. The rupee gained about thirty paise to close near ninety-four point nine five to the dollar, its best month since February. But Brent crude remains above one hundred and fourteen dollars a barrel.

In Tamil Nadu, Chief Minister Vijay has raised the age limit for government jobs by five years. General category candidates can now take the Group One civil services exam up to age thirty-seven, up from thirty-two.

In entertainment, Ranbir Kapoor's Ramayana Part One has crossed seventeen crore rupees in advance bookings, with more than three lakh tickets sold, six days before its November fifth release. And Shraddha Kapoor's Lavani musical Eetha released its trailer, ahead of a December fourth opening.

In cricket, New Zealand ended India's winning streak with an eighty-seven run victory in the fourth Twenty20 International in Auckland. Glenn Phillips hit one hundred and seven off forty-nine balls, with ten sixes, as New Zealand posted two hundred and fifty-nine for seven, their highest Twenty20 total. India managed only one hundred and seventy-two for nine, with Jacob Duffy taking four wickets. India still lead the series three to one, with the final match on Sunday.
