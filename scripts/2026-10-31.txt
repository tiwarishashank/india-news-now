This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Saturday, October thirty-first.

India's opposition held what it called a public impeachment of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar on Saturday. The symbolic event, postponed a day by rain, took place at Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge's home in New Delhi, with former Lok Sabha Speaker Meira Kumar presiding. Kharge moved a resolution listing six charges, including the deletion of thirteen point two crore voters during the special revision of electoral rolls, and demanded the commissioner be tried for treason. The exercise has no legal force. The INDIA bloc plans a march and rally at Delhi's Ramlila Maidan on Sunday, November first.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi marked Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel's birth anniversary in Gujarat, inaugurating and laying foundation stones for projects worth about four hundred and twenty-seven crore rupees around the Statue of Unity at Ekta Nagar.

Also on Saturday, Air Chief Marshal Ashutosh Dixit took charge as India's new Chief of Air Staff, succeeding Air Chief Marshal A. P. Singh. And in Punjab, the ruling Aam Aadmi Party released its first forty candidates for next year's assembly election. Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann will again contest from Dhuri.

In space news, ISRO's GSLV rocket launched the navigation satellite NVS-03 from Sriharikota on Friday, restoring India's NavIC navigation system to its minimum of four working satellites.

On the economy, the government has cut the export duty on petrol to zero for the next fifteen days, while raising the levy on diesel exports by three rupees a litre, to eleven rupees fifty paise. Domestic fuel taxes are unchanged.

Indian stocks ended the week higher. On Friday the Sensex rose three hundred and eight points to seventy-four thousand, four hundred and fifty-nine, and the Nifty gained one hundred and sixteen points to twenty-three thousand and fifty-three. Both indices rose nearly two percent for the week, led on Friday by IT and pharma shares. The rupee gained thirty-three paise to close at ninety-four point nine three to the dollar, while Brent crude stayed above one hundred and fourteen dollars a barrel.

In entertainment, Ranbir Kapoor's Ramayana is building strong buzz ahead of its November fifth release. Trade tracker Sacnilk says opening-day advance bookings in India have crossed twenty crore rupees, with more than four lakh tickets sold. The censor board has cleared the film with a U certificate and no cuts, at a running time of three hours and fifteen minutes. North America leads overseas pre-sales.

In cricket, India face New Zealand in the fifth and final Twenty20 International in Hamilton on Sunday. India have already won the series and lead three to one, after New Zealand's eighty-seven run win in Auckland on Friday. A five-match one-day series follows, starting Wednesday in Auckland.
