This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Sunday, November twenty-second.

Nepal's Prime Minister Balendra Shah, known as Balen, has arrived in New Delhi on his first bilateral foreign visit since taking office in March. The rapper-turned-politician is visiting at Prime Minister Narendra Modi's invitation. The two leaders will hold delegation-level talks on Monday, followed by a joint press conference. Mr. Shah is also set to meet President Droupadi Murmu and External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar.

Two road crashes have claimed sixteen lives. In Gujarat's Kutch district, seven people, including two serving Army personnel, their wives and three young children, were killed when their car collided with a truck on the Bhuj-Khavda highway on Saturday night. The families, from Uttarakhand's Garhwal region, were returning from the Rann of Kutch. Prime Minister Modi announced compensation of two lakh rupees for the next of kin of each person killed. And in Rajasthan, nine members of a family from Maharashtra's Dhule district died early Sunday when their vehicle rammed a parked truck on the Kota-Udaipur highway. Eight others were hurt.

The battle over voter lists continues. The Congress says Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi Vadra will lead a nationwide campaign against what the party calls vote theft, starting from Bhopal in December. In Uttar Pradesh, the B J P has decided not to give its booth agents Form Seven, which is used to seek deletion of names from electoral rolls, and will instead focus them on enrolling eligible voters.

Seven hundred Sikh pilgrims crossed the Attari-Wagah border into Pakistan on Sunday for the birth anniversary of Guru Nanak Dev on November twenty-fourth. Pakistan has issued visas to about three thousand Indian pilgrims for the occasion.

In business, foreign investors have poured a record one point two two lakh crore rupees into Indian stocks so far in November, the highest monthly inflow ever, after pulling money out in September and October. Indian markets will be shut on Tuesday for Guru Nanak Jayanti. The Sensex ended last week at eighty-three thousand, nine hundred and twenty, and the Nifty at twenty-five thousand, eight hundred and one. The rupee closed Friday at ninety-two point six seven to the dollar.

In entertainment, Ajay Devgn's Drishyam: The Conclusion is set to stream on Netflix from November twenty-seventh. Hindustan Times reports the film has earned about five hundred and eighty-nine crore rupees worldwide since its release in early October.

And in cricket, India are eight wickets from victory in the first Test in Wellington. Rishabh Pant hit one hundred and twenty-eight and Dhruv Jurel seventy-eight as India declared at seven hundred and twenty for nine, their highest Test total away from home and the highest by any team in a Test in New Zealand. Pant became the first Asian wicketkeeper to score Test centuries in all four SENA countries. Jasprit Bumrah then removed captain Tom Latham for a duck, and New Zealand closed day four at one hundred and nineteen for two, still two hundred and thirty-three runs behind.
