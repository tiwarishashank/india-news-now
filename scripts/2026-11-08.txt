Prime Minister Narendra Modi spent Diwali with security forces in Chhattisgarh's Bastar district, addressing personnel at a CoBRA operations base in Karanpur. He said that for the first time in forty-seven years, not a single CRPF jawan lost his life on duty over the past year. Modi said armed Naxals have been defeated, but warned that what he called "dimagi Naxals," or Naxals of the mind, remain a threat inside several organisations.

In Karnataka, Chief Minister D K Shivakumar attacked the Centre over ethanol blending in petrol. Samples were tested at sixty-seven locations after motorists complained, and Food and Civil Supplies Minister Rizwan Arshad says lab tests found ethanol content of up to thirty-one point two percent in an Indian Oil sample and thirty point five percent in a Hindustan Petroleum sample, well above the twenty percent limit. Arshad is due to release a detailed report on Monday.

On markets, Indian stocks ended slightly lower in Sunday's one-hour Diwali Muhurat trading session, which marks the start of Samvat twenty eighty-three. The Sensex slipped twenty-eight points to eighty thousand, nine hundred and ninety-nine, and the Nifty fell forty-four points to twenty-four thousand, nine hundred and eighty-six. That snapped a seven-session winning run, and it's the first time in nine years the benchmarks have closed lower on Diwali. Both indices rose between eight and nine percent last week. The rupee ended Friday at ninety-three point nine one to the dollar, its strongest level since April twenty-second.

The year just ended was also a record for share sales. Business Standard reports Indian companies raised two point two three trillion rupees through three hundred and seventy-five IPOs in Samvat twenty eighty-two. Jio Platforms was the biggest issue, raising more than thirty thousand crore rupees.

In entertainment, Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana Part One crossed two hundred crore rupees net in India on its fourth day. Hindustan Times reports the film earned about forty crore on Diwali by seven p.m., taking its domestic total to about two hundred and twenty-three crore.

In cricket, the BCCI says Rajat Patidar has pulled out of the India A squad for the Rising Stars Asia Cup to manage injuries. Ayush Badoni replaces him, and Rinku Singh is vice-captain under Tilak Varma. And at the Under-Nineteen Asia Cup in Oman, Pakistan beat India by three wickets. Arjun Rajput scored a century in India's two hundred and eight, but Hamza Zahoor's hundred took Pakistan home.
