The B J P and the Shiromani Akali Dal are back together. The two parties announced on Tuesday that they will contest all one hundred and seventeen seats in Punjab's twenty twenty-seven Assembly election as allies, six years after the Akali Dal quit the National Democratic Alliance over the now-repealed farm laws. Union Home Minister Amit Shah said the Akali Dal will be the senior partner, and its leader will be chief minister if the alliance wins. Seat-sharing talks are still under way. In Uttar Pradesh, the Samajwadi Party has offered the Congress twenty-six seats for its own twenty twenty-seven election, as the two opposition partners keep negotiating.

In Tamil Nadu, the Karthigai Deepam lamp was lit on Thiruparankundram hill in Madurai under heavy security and prohibitory orders, amid a long-running dispute over where on the hill the lamp should be lit. Police detained state B J P president Nainar Nagenthran and other leaders on their way to the hill. The Madras High Court's Madurai bench directed the state government to comply with the Supreme Court's order allowing the ritual, and to release those unlawfully detained.

In Uttar Pradesh's Kannauj district, four schoolchildren were killed and at least two dozen people hurt early Tuesday when a speeding truck rammed a school bus parked on the shoulder of the Agra-Lucknow Expressway. The bus had stopped so the children could use a toilet.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi spoke by phone with German Chancellor Friedrich Merz. The two leaders reviewed the India-Germany strategic partnership and discussed expanding trade and investment through the India-European Union free trade agreement, along with cooperation in defence and advanced technology.

Reuters reports that former Bangladesh Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina, who has lived in India since fleeing Dhaka in August twenty twenty-four, wants to return home on December sixteenth, Bangladesh's Victory Day, and has asked for a flight on the national carrier Biman. She faces a death sentence in Bangladesh over the deadly crackdown on the protests that ousted her.

Indian stock and currency markets were closed on Tuesday for Guru Nanak Jayanti and reopen on Wednesday. In a new report, S B I Research says the rupee has gained three point two percent since September even as the dollar strengthened. With markets expecting the Reserve Bank to raise rates at its December review, S B I says a quarter-point hike would be enough, warning that a larger move could trigger capital outflows and currency swings.

In entertainment, India went home empty-handed at the International Emmy Awards in New York. Vir Das's Netflix special Fool Volume lost in the comedy category, and SonyLIV's Real Kashmir Football Club lost the drama series award to France's Carême. Actor Vijay Sethupathi was the only Indian presenter at the ceremony.

And in cricket, fifteen-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi has hit the fastest double century in List A cricket. Playing for India A against the United Arab Emirates at the Asia Cup Rising Stars tournament in Doha, he reached two hundred in fifty-two balls and finished with two hundred and four off fifty-three, including twenty-eight sixes. India A chased down two hundred and fifty in just fourteen point two overs to win by seven wickets and reach the semifinals. Meanwhile, the senior team moves to Christchurch, where the second Test against New Zealand begins on Friday, November twenty-seventh, local time.
