The Union Cabinet has approved a fresh Constitution amendment bill that would raise the number of Lok Sabha seats by fifty percent in every state, while freezing each state's current share of seats until twenty fifty-one. The bill is tied to bringing in thirty-three percent reservation for women in Parliament and state legislatures. It comes after a similar bill was defeated in the Lok Sabha in April, when opposition parties said the promise of an equal increase for every state was not written into the text. Parliament holds a special sitting starting Monday, November thirtieth. The government needs a two-thirds majority, and the D M K's twenty-two Lok Sabha members could be crucial. M K Stalin says his party will back the bill only if Tamil Nadu's share of seats is protected and the current proportions stay frozen for another twenty-five years.

The Supreme Court has again ordered that the Karthigai Deepam lamp be lit on the disputed stone pillar atop Thiruparankundram hill in Madurai, this time on Saturday, November twenty-eighth. The court said officials had wilfully disobeyed its earlier order, when the lamp was lit at the usual spot instead, issued contempt notices, and suspended the temple's board of trustees.

The Tata Trusts have lost the first round of their court fight with Tata Sons. The Bombay High Court refused to block Monday's Tata Sons board meeting, or a chairman's casting vote, as the Trusts challenge the reappointment of N Chandrasekaran as executive chairman. Justice Abhay Ahuja said, "Let the meeting go on," but ruled that any decision will be subject to the court's final order. The next hearing is on January twenty-first.

On Dalal Street, stocks fell sharply as oil prices jumped after a Houthi attack on a Saudi Aramco facility. The Sensex dropped nine hundred and eighty-nine points, or one point one seven percent, to eighty-three thousand, three hundred and thirty-nine. The Nifty lost two hundred and seventy-seven points to twenty-five thousand, six hundred and seven. Both indices ended a four-week winning streak. The rupee fell sixty paise, settling at ninety-two point nine eight to the dollar. India's economic growth figures for July to September are due on Monday.

In entertainment, Ramayana: Part One is on the verge of one thousand crore rupees net in India. Its total stood at about nine hundred and ninety-two crore after its fourth Thursday, and it was expected to cross the mark on Friday.

Sri Lanka A chased down three hundred and eighteen in forty-one point three overs to win the semifinal by eight wickets, with centuries from both openers. Rohit Sharma is back as Mumbai Indians captain for next year's I P L, nearly three years after he was replaced by Hardik Pandya.
