Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge on Friday wrote to Prime Minister Narendra Modi urging an all-party meeting on the Centre's delimitation proposals, and warning against any attempt to push through a far-reaching Constitutional Amendment Bill without wider consultation. In his letter, Kharge flagged what he called an unprecedented sixty-four-day delay in proroguing Parliament after the Monsoon Session was adjourned sine die on August thirteenth. He said this was his fourth such request since April, after a delimitation amendment bill failed to win a two-thirds majority in the Lok Sabha in April. Separately, Union Minister Rajnath Singh accused the Congress and its allies of seeking a Bangladesh-style regime change through protests in the capital. The Congress hit back, citing former President Pranab Mukherjee's line that the Opposition's job is to oppose, expose and depose.

In Delhi, police detained Cockroach Janta Party founder Abhijeet Dipke and AISA chief Neha Bohra on their way to a Lehra Do Tiranga protest at Connaught Place. About six thousand police personnel were deployed, Central Park was closed, and Dipke had been served a notice barring the gathering. The Supreme Court also issued show-cause notices to Shiv Sena U.B.T. M.P. Sanjay Raut and journalist Nikhil Wagle in contempt proceedings over remarks about the Chief Justice and the judiciary.

Indian stocks closed higher for a second straight day, led by I.T. The Sensex rose four hundred and fourteen points, or about zero point six percent, to seventy-four thousand, seven hundred and twenty-nine. The Nifty gained one hundred and forty-nine points to end at twenty-three thousand, one hundred and forty-six. For the week, the Sensex was up about three point one percent and the Nifty about two point eight percent. Tech Mahindra jumped more than eight percent after reporting a thirty-three percent rise in quarterly net profit to about fifteen hundred and eighty-six crore rupees, and announcing a one-for-one bonus plus an interim dividend. T.C.S., Infosys and H.C.L. Tech also gained. The rupee settled near ninety-four point nine six against the U.S. dollar, stronger than Thursday's close of ninety-five point zero five.

In entertainment, Rajinikanth's Jailer Two took about sixty crore rupees on its opening day in India. And at a Ramayana trailer launch in Bengaluru, Yash, who plays Ravana, defended the film's reported four-thousand-crore budget as needed for global attention, and urged audiences not to pull the project down. The first part, starring Ranbir Kapoor and Sai Pallavi, is due November fifth.

In cricket, the B.C.C.I. added Bhuvneshwar Kumar to India's squad for Saturday's fifth T twenty against West Indies in Bengaluru after Arshdeep Singh hurt his bowling shoulder in Hyderabad. A call on Arshdeep will come before the series decider at the Chinnaswamy Stadium.
