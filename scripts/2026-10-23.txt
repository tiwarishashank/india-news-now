This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Friday, October twenty-third.

India's foreign ministry says tension on the border with China flares up from time to time when Chinese forces depart from established patrolling patterns. Spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal spoke on Friday, a day after the chief of the Indo-Tibetan Border Police, Shatrujeet Kapur, said face-offs with Chinese troops had taken place at several locations in Arunachal Pradesh in recent days, and were still continuing. Jaiswal said both sides were using diplomatic and military channels to avoid miscalculation, and referred specific questions to the Defence Ministry. The Congress party has attacked Prime Minister Narendra Modi over the face-offs.

The row over the revision of electoral rolls spilled onto the streets of Delhi. Police stopped and detained protesters marching toward the Election Commission's headquarters, demanding an end to the special revision and the impeachment of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. In Tamil Nadu, parties including the ruling T V K and the D M K walked out of a meeting called by the state's chief electoral officer on the coming revision.

In Ladakh, activist Sonam Wangchuk accused the central government of going back on its assurances, after talks planned for Thursday in Delhi did not take place. He now says Ladakh should seek full statehood.

Indian stocks fell for a fourth straight session as Brent crude climbed to about one hundred and fourteen dollars a barrel amid Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia. The Sensex slipped about one hundred and twenty-six points to close at seventy-three thousand, one hundred and twenty-four. The Nifty fell nearly eighty-eight points to twenty-two thousand, six hundred. Smaller stocks took a bigger hit, with the Nifty Smallcap index down almost three percent. The rupee weakened for a second day, closing at about ninety-four point nine three to the dollar.

The three point two billion dollar public offering of Jio Platforms closed with bids for more than twenty times the shares on offer. It became the first share sale anywhere to draw more than ten million applications. The shares are due to list on October twenty-eighth.

In Mumbai, police are investigating an email sent to Mayor Ritu Tawde's office that warned of attacks on her office, on Amitabh Bachchan's home, and on Diljit Dosanjh's concert planned for November twenty-seventh. The sender claimed links to a Khalistani group. Police searched the civic headquarters and found nothing suspicious.

In cricket, India lead New Zealand one-nil in their five-match T twenty series after their highest successful run chase in T twenty internationals on Thursday, overhauling Glenn Phillips' unbeaten eighty-two. The second match is on Saturday at Hagley Oval in Christchurch. Shreyas Iyer's side will be looking to build on Sanju Samson's sixty-seven and Naman Dhir's unbeaten forty-two off fifteen balls.
