The Union Cabinet has overhauled the Agnipath scheme. Chief of Defence Staff General N S Raja Subramani said Agniveers who finish their four-year tenure will still see the top twenty-five percent retained on merit. The remaining seventy-five percent will now be offered a two-year second engagement, with a path to join the armed forces as regular soldiers if they meet objective selection criteria. Pay during the extension rises to forty-three thousand five hundred rupees a month in year five and forty-seven thousand five hundred in year six. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh called it a second tour of duty aimed at a younger, fitter force. More than three lakh Agniveers are currently serving across the three services, with the first Navy batch completing its initial tenure this month.

Indian stocks rose for a fifth straight session. The Sensex gained three hundred and eighty-four points, or half a percent, to close at seventy-seven thousand, three hundred and one. The Nifty climbed one hundred and fifty points to twenty-three thousand, nine hundred and sixty-one. Midcaps and smallcaps led the advance, while IT was the only major sectoral loser. The rupee settled seventeen paise stronger at ninety-four point five eight to the dollar, helped by softer crude and overseas buying. Separately, India's services PMI rose to fifty-five point eight in October, a four-month high, as domestic demand strengthened and cost pressures eased to their lowest in more than six years, according to the HSBC survey.

Iran has exempted merchant ships from India, China, Iraq and Qatar from Strait of Hormuz restrictions, an Iranian lawmaker said, citing easier access to medicines from India.

In entertainment, Ranbir Kapoor's Ramayana Part One is set for a worldwide theatrical release on November fifth in Hindi and several South Indian languages. Alia Bhatt and Neetu Kapoor attended the Mumbai premiere. Advance bookings have been softer than trade expectations for a film reported to cost hundreds of crores, with audiences taking a wait-and-watch approach. Producer Namit Malhotra confirmed Part Two has shifted to twenty twenty-eight and floated the idea of a trilogy depending on how the first film lands.

In cricket, Virat Kohli became the first batter in one-day history to score thirteen thousand runs at number three, during India's first ODI against New Zealand at Eden Park in Auckland. Kohli made seventy-four off fifty-one balls as India chased a rain-revised target and won by five wickets to lead the five-match series one-nil. New Zealand were bowled out for one hundred and eighty-five, with Arshdeep Singh taking four wickets.
