Cyclone Arnab is bearing down on India's east coast. The India Meteorological Department says it will strengthen into a very severe cyclonic storm and cross the coast near Yanam or Kakinada in Andhra Pradesh around noon on Wednesday, October twenty-eighth, with winds gusting to one hundred and forty kilometers an hour. Andhra Pradesh has identified about five hundred and seventy thousand vulnerable people for evacuation, and red alerts are out for seven districts. Odisha has shut schools and colleges in five districts on Tuesday and Wednesday.

A court in Sultanpur, Uttar Pradesh, has acquitted opposition leader Rahul Gandhi in a two thousand eighteen defamation case over remarks about Amit Shah. Gandhi also told Delhi traders, who had asked him to postpone the INDIA bloc's November first rally against alleged vote theft, that the protests would stop in thirty seconds if Prime Minister Narendra Modi sacked Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. The B J P, meanwhile, protested across states over Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge's remarks about the prime minister's late father.

In Tamil Nadu, former D M K education minister Anbil Mahesh Poyyamozhi was arrested hours after the Madras High Court rejected his anticipatory bail plea, in an alleged scam involving party funds collected from private schools.

Indian stocks snapped a four-day losing streak. The Sensex rose four hundred and eighty-three points to close at seventy-three thousand, six hundred and seven, and the Nifty gained one hundred and fifty-three points to twenty-two thousand, seven hundred and fifty-three, led by metal, telecom and healthcare shares. But the rupee weakened for a third straight session to about ninety-five point two to the dollar, with Brent crude near one hundred and fifteen dollars a barrel.

In entertainment, Vijay Deverakonda and Rashmika Mandanna have filed a complaint with cybercrime authorities in Hyderabad over what their team calls fabricated content and targeted harassment, after trolling that followed their appearance on Koffee With Karan. And police sources told The Indian Express that the gunmen in the October eighteenth attack on Tiger Shroff were first sent after Aamir Khan, but switched targets because he was hard to reach.

In cricket, India's women have sealed their one-day series against Zimbabwe. In Vadodara on Sunday, Harmanpreet Kaur made one hundred and twenty-six, her eighth one-day century, and Deepti Sharma hit an unbeaten one hundred and six as India posted three hundred and twenty-eight for six. Vaishnavi Sharma took four wickets as Zimbabwe were all out for two hundred and seven, giving India a one hundred and twenty-one run win and an unbeatable two-nil lead.
