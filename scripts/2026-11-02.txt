A severe cyclone is bearing down on India's east coast tonight. The India Meteorological Department says Cyclone Murasu is expected to cross the south Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Narsapur, close to Ongole, between eleven thirty Monday night and four on Tuesday morning, local time. Winds could reach one hundred and ten kilometres an hour, gusting to one hundred and twenty, with a storm surge of up to two metres in low-lying areas. Andhra Pradesh disaster officials say about two thousand people have been moved so far, with plans to evacuate some thirty thousand. At least nineteen domestic flights were cancelled at three airports in the state, and schools in Chennai were shut as heavy rain hit the city.

In Delhi, the Supreme Court will allow certified green firecrackers in the capital region for Diwali, but only on November seventh and eighth, from six to seven in the morning and eight to ten at night. The year-round ban returns on November ninth. Meanwhile, Home Minister Amit Shah joined a BJP march in Hyderabad, a day after the opposition's big rally in Delhi against the Chief Election Commissioner.

Indian stocks had a strong start to the week. The Sensex jumped one thousand, two hundred and thirty-three points, or one point seven percent, to close at seventy-five thousand, six hundred and ninety-three. The Nifty rose three hundred and eighty-eight points to twenty-three thousand, four hundred and forty-one. Falling crude oil prices helped. The rupee slipped slightly, ending near ninety-four point nine to the dollar.

The big mover was Reliance Industries, which surged nearly twelve percent, its best day in more than six years. The company reported record quarterly revenue of three point six two lakh crore rupees, as refining margins hit historic highs, and its market value climbed past seventeen and a half lakh crore rupees.

In entertainment, Shah Rukh Khan turned sixty-one and marked the day by releasing "Tu Mil Jaye," the first song from his action film King. The track, composed by Sachin-Jigar, is sung by Arijit Singh and Madhubanti Bagchi, with an English rap by Ed Sheeran, and features Deepika Padukone. King, directed by Siddharth Anand, opens in theatres in December.

In cricket, India are preparing for Wednesday's first one-day international against New Zealand at Eden Park in Auckland, the start of a five-match series. Coach Gautam Gambhir called it probably the most inexperienced bowling attack in one-day cricket, with Jasprit Bumrah rested. Rishabh Pant failed a fitness test on Sunday and will get another chance before the Test series begins on November nineteenth.
