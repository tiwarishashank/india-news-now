External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar says a trade deal with the United States is approaching the finishing line and could be sealed soon. Speaking at the Hindustan Times Leadership Summit, he said the bulk of the issues have been sorted out, and that many of the remaining ones are not so much at India's end. Prime Minister Narendra Modi is likely to visit the US for the G twenty Summit next month, where he may meet President Donald Trump.

Pakistan says its climate minister, Musadik Masood Malik, will attend a Shanghai Cooperation Organisation dialogue in New Delhi from November twentieth to twenty-second. It would be the first visit by a Pakistani minister to India in more than three years.

The CBI says it has searched thirty-three locations across six states in the Mahadev online betting app case, including Delhi, Mumbai, Nagpur and Raipur. Officials say they seized phones, digital devices and documents pointing to mule bank accounts and hawala channels. The searches followed disclosures by Sourabh Chandrakar, one of the app's main promoters, after his extradition from Oman.

In Assam, post-mortems are being carried out on twenty-six people killed when a minibus collided with a container truck near Kaziranga. Seven of the eighteen injured remain critical.

On markets, Indian stocks ended the week on a weak note. On Friday, rate hike fears after October's inflation data pulled the Sensex down three hundred and nineteen points to eighty-one thousand, five hundred and ninety-two, and the Nifty down one hundred and fifty-five points to twenty-five thousand, one hundred and forty-four. The rupee closed at about ninety-four point two to the dollar. Separately, the food ministry has opened an online portal where traders, retailers and millers must declare rice and paddy stocks every Friday, a move aimed at curbing hoarding as rice prices climb.

In entertainment, Hindustan Times reports Ramayana Part One has sailed past eleven hundred crore rupees worldwide in nine days, overtaking Pathaan and Kalki twenty eight ninety-eight A D.

And in cricket, India have won the Under-Nineteen Asia Cup in an extraordinary finish against Pakistan in Oman. Pakistan made three hundred and thirty-seven for nine, led by a century from Usman Khan. India were two hundred and fifty-two for eight in the forty-second over, needing eighty-seven more. Manal Chauhan made forty-seven, and number ten Mohit Ulva, unbeaten on forty-nine, hit the winning shot off the very last ball. India won by one wicket, reversing last year's final defeat. Prime Minister Modi said he was proud of the young cricketers.
