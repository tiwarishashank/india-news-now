This is India News Today with Nisha. It's Sunday, October 18.

Kolkata police have arrested seven people, including the secretary and vice president of the organizing committee, after a stampede at a Durga Puja pandal killed nineteen people and injured twenty-five. The crush happened on Saturday evening at the Chorbagan pandal in north Kolkata, when rain drove crowds inside and an announcement allegedly warned that the structure could collapse. Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari has ordered an independent probe headed by a sitting High Court judge and announced compensation of twenty-five lakh rupees for each victim's family. Union Home Minister Amit Shah visited the injured on Sunday.

In Madhya Pradesh, at least fourteen people were killed and more than forty injured when a truck loaded with paddy ploughed into devotees near the Sheetla Mata temple in Gwalior, at about one thirty on Sunday morning. The driver and the truck's owner have been arrested, and Chief Minister Mohan Yadav removed a senior city police officer and suspended eight others over alleged negligence.

In politics, the Congress party is demanding that the special revision of electoral rolls be suspended and that Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar step down, even after the Election Commission agreed to share readable voter rolls with political parties. Meanwhile, U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio is set to visit Mumbai and Jamnagar this week to discuss trade, energy, and investment.

In business, state-run oil companies have barred ethanol made from sugarcane juice and sugar syrup from their latest tender for blending in petrol, as the government moves to protect domestic sugar supplies. YES Bank reported a sixty-five percent jump in quarterly profit, to about one thousand and eighty crore rupees, though the figure does not yet reflect a court ruling on its written-off bonds. Markets are closed for the weekend. On Friday, the Sensex closed at seventy-four thousand, seven hundred and twenty-nine, up four hundred and thirteen points, and the Nifty at twenty-three thousand, one hundred and forty-six. The rupee ended near ninety-four point nine six to the dollar.

At the box office, Rajinikanth's Jailer Two has crossed three hundred crore rupees worldwide in its first three days, according to trade trackers.

In cricket, rain washed out the fifth and deciding T twenty against West Indies in Bengaluru, leaving the series level at two-all. India had raced to one hundred and eighteen for five in under eight overs before play was called off. West Indies captain Shai Hope was named player of the series for his two hundred and sixty-one runs. India now heads to New Zealand for an all-format tour beginning October twenty-second. And the Asian Para Games open today in Nagoya, Japan, with Pramod Bhagat and Simran Sharma carrying India's flag.
