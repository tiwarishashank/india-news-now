The Supreme Court says banks and financial institutions must compensate victims of digital fraud if an investigation finds lapses in due diligence. The court said lenders need safeguards to flag suspicious transactions before money leaves an account, and should alert nearby police stations about unusual activity. The bench also said it would examine the Reserve Bank of India's proposed new know-your-customer rules.
