U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio visited Jamnagar, Gujarat, on Wednesday, touring Reliance's refinery complex. Rubio said India is one of the few places in the world that can refine Venezuela's heavy crude, and called energy diversification a shared goal. In an interview with The Indian Express, he said a trade deal is weeks, not months, away, with ninety-nine percent of the work done. President Donald Trump also said the two sides are pretty close. Rubio sought to reassure New Delhi about Washington's ties with Pakistan, saying America's relationship with any country will never come at the expense of its relationship with India.

In Delhi, a magistrate on Wednesday ordered the release of Cockroach Janata Party leaders Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka. The two were taken into custody after trying to hold a protest march at Jantar Mantar on October fourteenth. They are demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over alleged voter deletions during the revision of electoral rolls. The order came hours after they withdrew a petition in the Delhi High Court.

Delhi's air quality slipped into the poor category the day after Dussehra, and authorities have put the first stage of the region's anti-pollution action plan into effect across Delhi and the National Capital Region.

Indian stocks fell for a second straight session. The Sensex lost one hundred and forty-three points to close at seventy-three thousand, seven hundred and ten, and the Nifty ended at twenty-two thousand, eight hundred and eleven. Both indices opened higher before banking and financial stocks dragged them down, while IT shares such as Infosys and TCS gained. The rupee strengthened forty-six paise to close at ninety-four point four six to the dollar.

At the box office, Rajinikanth's Jailer Two earned about forty-seven crore rupees net in India on its first Tuesday, which was also Dussehra, according to the tracker Sacnilk. Its worldwide gross now stands at about five hundred and forty-two crore. In the Tiger Shroff firing case, the three accused have been remanded to police custody until October thirty-first.

Vummidi Ethiraj, the Chennai jeweller who crafted the Sengol installed in the new Parliament, has died at ninety-nine. Prime Minister Narendra Modi expressed his condolences.

In cricket, India open a five-match T twenty series against New Zealand on Thursday at Hagley Oval in Christchurch, led by Shreyas Iyer. Bhuvneshwar Kumar returns to India's T twenty side after nearly four years. New Zealand captain Mitchell Santner said Bhuvneshwar will love the conditions there. The two teams last met in the T twenty World Cup final in March, which India won by ninety-six runs.
